OUR PURPOSE & THE PERSON BEHIND IT

About DrillDeck.

A focused, flexible practice space for people preparing for Karnataka state-level exams.

THE IDEA

Make every practice session count.

DrillDeck brings exam practice, revision and performance review into one clear experience. Instead of one fixed mock paper, you can choose a quick practice session or build a custom test around the subjects and topics you want to work on.

The goal is simple: make it easier to start, stay focused while you practise, and learn something useful from every attempt.

01

Practice that fits your plan

Quick Test lets you set the number of questions, difficulty and time for a General Knowledge test, or select subjects for a combined paper when those question banks are available.

Custom Exam gives you more control: choose a paper, categories, subcategories and topics, then set the difficulty, question count and duration.

Explore Quick Test →
02

Designed for useful review

Timed, one-question-at-a-time tests include answer navigation, mark-for-review, a question palette and automatic submission when time runs out.

After submission, review answers and explanations, see your score and subject breakdown, and use the feedback to guide your next session. Correct answers earn +1, incorrect answers −0.25, and unanswered questions 0.

Build a custom exam →
03

Your results, your choice

Detailed results stay in this browser and can be exported as a JSON backup. Accounts are not required. Chat is shared, and leaderboard participation is optional: a score is shared only when you opt in.

Public chat messages and scores are visible to other visitors. Do not include private or sensitive information in a chat message or public display name.

04

A growing question library

The question bank is organized into subject datasets and validated before questions are used. General Knowledge includes current affairs, general science and geography material.

Kannada, English and Computer are available as selectable combined-paper subjects, but a test can start only when question sets for the selected subjects have been added.
